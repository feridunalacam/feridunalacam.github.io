02

Using the app

Sync provides interval routines, a timer, music controls and saved activity for movement, study and focus. The existing local features and guest mode are free. When enabled, Free accounts and guests may see a labelled Discover banner and a preloaded full-screen ad at a new session start or after choosing Continue once a session has ended. Full-screen ads are skipped if not already loaded, and do not appear during running or paused sessions, work/rest intervals, app-controlled music playback or audio synchronization. Frequency limits apply: at least two distinct, meaningful app actions before a first or subsequent full-screen ad, and at least two minutes between full-screen ads. Unavailable ads or declined privacy choices do not remove the core timer, routines or music controls. A Sync account adds authentication and keeps each account’s device data separate.

You control what you enter into routines, notes, profile details and saved posts. Only import or share content you are entitled to use. Spotify and other external services require their own eligible account, access and terms.

03

Sync Pro

Pro adds an ad-free experience, private cloud backup and device synchronization for routines, recent activity and profile details. Verified Pro access suppresses banner and full-screen ad preparation, requests and display; managing privacy choices remains available where required. Imported audio and photos remain device-local. Synchronization needs an internet connection and pauses during an active workout.

Monthly and annual options unlock the same Pro features. The available plan, billing period and localized price shown in store checkout apply to your purchase. Any trial or introductory offer is available only when expressly shown in that checkout.

A purchase made while signed in is connected to that Sync account and verified on the backend. Sync Pro can also be bought without an account: that purchase is connected to your store account on this device and unlocks unlimited routines and no ads, while cloud backup and device sync need a Sync account. When you later sign in, the purchase is connected to that Sync account. If verification is delayed, use Refresh access or Restore purchases. Contact support if the purchase remains unresolved instead of repeatedly buying the same plan.

Restoring purchases in another Sync account can move Pro access to that account and remove it from the previous account. Private data is not moved or merged. An existing store receipt can also cause a transfer during a purchase. Confirm the signed-in account before proceeding.

Deleting Sync or deleting your Sync account is not a cancellation instruction to Apple or Google. Cancelling renewal and deleting an account are separate actions.

06

Your records and deletion

Cloud backups are private account records, not public posts. Keep copies of important information and local media: local audio files need to be selected again on another device, and conflicting backup versions require a choice before synchronization continues.

An expired Pro membership or a transfer to another Sync account does not automatically delete the original account’s stored cloud backup. That backup remains attached to its original account, and Pro access is needed to use cloud backup features. Local app features remain free. A Free account may again be eligible for the described banner and session-transition ad placements when advertising is enabled and privacy, access and frequency checks allow them.

Deleting a Sync account removes its related cloud application records, but does not automatically cancel or remove records held by the purchasing store or RevenueCat. See the privacy notice for deletion boundaries and contact options.
