02

Account information

For email/password registration and sign-in, Sync sends your email address and password to Supabase Auth for authentication, email confirmation and password recovery. Supabase manages your account identifier and authentication records. Native app sessions are kept in secure device storage.

Supabase also processes account-associated authentication, session and request records to provide sign-in, protect accounts and operate the service. These records can include account or session identifiers, IP addresses, browser or client information, request times, routes and status, request timing, and IP-derived approximate country or region. Sync uses this operational information for authentication, security and service reliability, rather than advertising targeting. Provider-managed logs have their own retention and access arrangements, separate from the app records described below.

Guest mode keeps the core timer, routines, music controls and activity available without creating a Sync account. Available sign-in choices are shown in the app. If you choose Google or Sign in with Apple, that provider sends authentication credentials and account information, such as a provider identifier and email address, to Supabase Auth to sign you in. Sync does not receive your Google or Apple password. Google-supplied name and profile-image URLs can also be retained in Supabase Auth as authentication metadata. The native Sign in with Apple flow requests your email address, not your name.

For native Sign in with Apple, Sync can send Apple’s one-use authorization code to its authenticated server to obtain a revocation credential. The server checks that it belongs to the signed-in account and stores the refresh token encrypted, linked to your Sync account and Apple identifier. This credential is used to revoke the Apple connection when you delete your Sync account. It is separate from Apple Music permission, is not included in routines or Pro backups, and is not sent to advertising services.

03

Private cloud backups

Sync Pro can back up your routines, recent activity history, bio, social links and your own saved routine posts. Activity is normally kept to the latest 100 saved sessions; imported existing records can be merged. Session records can include dates, elapsed time, completed rounds and skipped intervals.

Routine backups can include selected track, playlist and artwork metadata or remote audio links. They do not make your posts or routines public. The backup is tied to your Sync account and access is checked on the server.

When this device and another device both have changes, Sync asks which copy to keep. A local recovery copy is kept before a cloud restore. Synchronization pauses during an active workout.

04

What stays on your device

Imported audio files and profile photos are copied into the app’s device storage. The current app does not upload these files as part of cloud backup. Local file references are excluded from the backup, so you may need to select the files again on another device.

On supported iOS versions, an optional Apple Music player uses Apple’s MusicKit permission and account capabilities to show the playlists or tracks you request, search Apple’s catalog and play music you choose. It is available to guests and Free accounts without a Sync account or Pro subscription. Playback begins when you choose Play; it works independently of the interval timer rather than following timer phases.

Apple manages MusicKit credentials. Sync keeps a device-local connection marker; the current player’s library results, search results and playback queue stay in app memory and are not added to routines or Pro cloud backups. Music selection is not sent into advertising. On supported iOS 26.4 and later, Sync excludes its new playback queues from Recently Played; other Apple processing and system versions follow Apple’s service behavior and settings.

Spotify and other supported services open in their own apps or your browser in the public-release configuration. External links do not give Sync access to those accounts or libraries, or control of their playback. The destination processes requests under its own terms and privacy notice.

Some earlier versions offered native music connections. Spotify access and refresh tokens were kept in secure device storage and were not part of cloud backups. Apple’s MusicKit managed Apple Music credentials; Sync kept only a device-local connection marker. Saved song or playlist metadata can remain in routines and account-linked Pro backups. Earlier device-local connection records can remain until cleared by account cleanup; you can separately revoke provider access through the provider’s controls or iOS Settings. Connecting a service did not upload a full music library or media files.

Saving to Discover is private saved content, not online community publishing. Sharing through your device’s share sheet or opening an external music service or sound library sends information to the destination you choose.

Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable service arrangements and policies. Their documents explain their processing, infrastructure security and retention; they do not replace this notice.

06

In-app advertising and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, Free accounts and guests may see a clearly labelled banner in Discover and a preloaded full-screen ad when starting a new session or choosing Continue after a session has ended. Full-screen ads do not appear during running or paused sessions, work/rest intervals, app-controlled music playback or audio synchronization. Ads are also suppressed while the Apple Music player is open, permission or music loading is in progress, or its selected playback queue is still owned by Sync, including while paused. An ad that is not already loaded at an eligible transition is skipped; it will not appear later during the session. The timer, routines, music controls and guest mode remain available if ads are unavailable or you decline a privacy choice.

Full-screen ads are limited to one per two minutes. Sync also requires at least two distinct, meaningful app actions before the first or a subsequent full-screen ad. The configured production full-screen units allow text, image and rich-media ads, with video disabled. Sync requests muted playback from the ad SDK with its volume set to zero. Google provides each creative’s duration and closing controls.

Verified Sync Pro access suppresses preparation, requests and display for both banner and full-screen ad placements. Ad preparation and requests are also suppressed while account access is unresolved. Privacy choices remain available independently of Pro access.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) provides the banner and full-screen ads, and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable advertising privacy choices. Sync requests non-personalized ads. When advertising is enabled on iOS, Google’s User Messaging Platform may present an explanation and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission request. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising measurement across other companies’ apps and websites and to limit repeated ads. Google will not receive the IDFA through Sync without your permission. Declining does not prevent use of Sync’s core features. Other SDK processing described below may continue, subject to applicable privacy choices. Non-personalized does not mean no data is processed. The SDK can process device or app-bounded identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad impressions/interactions, product interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement and service protection. Its publisher first-party ID default is retained; Sync does not claim to disable that identifier.

Sync does not pass your account identifier or email, routines, activity records, profile, private cloud-backup content, music metadata or connection credentials into the ads integration. These private records are not used by Sync as ad-targeting inputs. Google’s SDK processing is separate from the account and backup records described above. See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure (external website) and Google’s privacy notice (external website).

The app checks UMP choices before initializing ads for an eligible placement. Where Google indicates that privacy options are required, use Profile → Preferences → Ad privacy choices to review or change them. When advertising is enabled, this choice is available independently of a Sync account or Pro status and does not itself initialize ads. Return when no workout, music playback or audio synchronization is active. Device advertising/privacy settings are also available through your operating system. If a choice is unavailable or you need help with provider-held records, contact the privacy address below; deleting a Sync account does not automatically erase Google-held advertising records.

07

Subscription records and transfers

The Sync backend keeps an account-linked Pro access record, its expiry, purchase store, environment and update time. Private billing support records keep subscription synchronization status and timestamps, plus processed notification identifiers and processing times to prevent duplicate updates.

The backend does not store raw RevenueCat webhook payloads or payment-card details in those records. Billing error messages do not intentionally log raw notifications or access tokens. Hosting and authentication providers can still process operational request metadata.

Restoring a store purchase in a different Sync account can move Pro access and remove it from the previous account. Private routines, history, profiles and cloud backups remain with their original account; they are not transferred.

08

Retention and account deletion

Cloud backups and billing support records have no automatic time-based deletion schedule. An expired or transferred Pro subscription stops that account’s access to Pro backup features but does not immediately delete its stored backup. A later successful backup replaces the previous cloud snapshot.

Use Profile → Your account → Delete account to request deletion in the app. You must have signed in recently and type the requested confirmation. Successful deletion removes the sign-in account, its related profile, cloud-backup, subscription-access and private subscription-notification records, and any owned avatar objects. The encrypted Apple revocation credential has no automatic expiry-based cleanup: it remains until replaced by a later successful registration or removed with the Sync account. Signing out or revoking access in Apple settings does not itself delete this server record. Encrypted Apple revocation credentials are removed with the account. Where a valid credential is available, the server attempts to revoke the Sign in with Apple connection. If no credential is available or Apple cannot complete the request, account deletion still proceeds and Sync shows instructions to remove the connection in Apple settings. The limited security record described below remains. The app also attempts to clear this device’s account-scoped cache and stored connection credentials, and reports if that cleanup cannot be completed.

For each account, we retain a limited security record after deletion containing only its system-generated account identifier (UUID) and whether it was classified for normal production billing or restricted review/test billing. We retain this record indefinitely to prevent the same identifier from being reassigned between test and real billing environments. This record contains no email address, name, profile content, routines, activity, cloud-backup content, connection credentials or payment details. It does not keep the deleted sign-in account active, restore its data or preserve subscription access.

Deleting a Sync account does not cancel a store subscription or automatically erase RevenueCat or store records. Manage your subscription in the store first. Contact feridun.alacam@gmail.com for help with a privacy request that also concerns service-provider records.

Copies of imported media and some device-only markers can remain on the device. Copies on other devices, provider-managed operational logs and infrastructure backups are not subject to a promise of immediate physical erasure. Provider retention depends on the service, security needs and applicable obligations; consult the linked provider policies rather than assuming one fixed period for every record.

Support correspondence is used to respond, investigate and follow up on your request. Contact the support address if you want help reviewing or removing that correspondence.

Your choices and privacy requests

Contact feridun.alacam@gmail.com for questions about your information or to request access, a copy, correction or deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent where processing depends on it, or complain to your relevant data-protection authority.

We may ask for information needed to verify that the request concerns your account and to protect other users’ records. State which account and information your request concerns, without sending your password or access tokens.

You can disconnect the Apple Music player in Sync and separately revoke its system permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music. Disconnecting in Sync does not itself revoke the iOS permission. Manage externally opened music services through their own apps or settings. Revoking permission does not remove music metadata already saved by earlier versions in routines or a Pro backup. Sign in with Apple and Apple Music permissions are separate choices.

To stop future Pro renewals, use the purchasing store’s subscription settings. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate choices.
