02

Signing in or recovering an account

I haven’t received a confirmation or reset email.

Check your spam folder and the email address you entered. Open the newest link on the device where you requested it. If a link has expired or cannot be used, request a new one. Contact support if the message still does not arrive.

What happens when I sign out?

Sync returns to a separate guest space. Account records remain separate from guest records and from another signed-in account. Existing device guest records can be imported explicitly from Profile once per device installation.

03

Purchases, restoration and transfers

I paid, but Pro is not active.

Check which Sync account is signed in. In Profile, open Sync Pro and try Refresh access. If needed, use Restore purchases with the store account that made the original purchase. Avoid buying the same plan again while verification is pending.

Can I restore Pro to a different Sync account?

Yes. Restore with the purchasing store account to move Pro access to the signed-in Sync account. The previous account loses access. Private routines, history, profile and backups stay with the original account.

Sign into the original account to use its private records. Review the signed-in account before restoring. See RevenueCat restore behavior (external website).

How do I stop renewal?

Open Profile → Sync Pro → Manage subscription, or use your purchasing store’s subscription settings. For Apple purchases, follow Apple’s cancellation steps (external website). Deleting the app or your Sync account does not cancel the store subscription.

How do I ask about an Apple refund?

Start with Apple’s billing and subscriptions support (external website). The store determines the available request process and eligibility.

04

Cloud backups and local audio

Another device has a different backup.

If both copies have edits, Profile shows a backup conflict. Use the cloud copy to replace this device’s account records, or use this device’s copy to replace the cloud backup. Read the confirmation carefully. A device recovery copy is kept before a cloud restore.

My audio or photo is missing on another device.

Imported audio and profile photos remain on the device where they were selected. Backup sync carries routine and track metadata, not the local media files. Select the files again on the new device. The optional Apple Music player keeps its current library results and queue in app memory; these are not part of Pro backups. Spotify and other external services are managed in their own apps. Connection credentials are not part of Pro backups.

What happens after Pro expires?

Local features remain available. Pro cloud access stops, but the stored cloud backup is not automatically deleted just because the subscription expired. See retention and deletion.

05

Ads and privacy choices

Where can ads appear?

When enabled, Free accounts and guests may see one labelled banner placement in Discover, outside its scrolling feed. A preloaded full-screen ad can appear when starting a new session or choosing Continue after a session has ended. The app does not show ads during running or paused sessions, work/rest intervals, app-controlled music playback or audio synchronization. Ads are also suppressed while the Apple Music player is open, permission or loading is in progress, or Sync owns its queue, including while paused; the banner is also hidden during search and detail views. Verified Pro access suppresses preparation, requests and display for both formats, while advertising privacy choices remain available.

Full-screen ads need at least two distinct, meaningful app actions before the first or another can appear, with at least two minutes between them. Only an ad already loaded at that transition can show. An unavailable or late-loading ad is skipped and does not pop up after the session begins. Unavailable ads or declined privacy choices do not remove the free timer, routines, music controls or guest mode.

Can full-screen ads interrupt a round or play sound?

Full-screen ads are shown only at the session transitions described above, not inside a work or rest interval or when resuming a paused session. The configured production full-screen units allow text, image and rich-media ads, with video disabled. Sync requests muted playback from the ad SDK with volume set to zero. Google provides the creative’s duration and closing controls.

How do I change advertising privacy choices?

Where UMP indicates that privacy options are required, open Profile → Preferences → Ad privacy choices. Guests and Pro users can use this option too. Return when no workout, music playback or audio synchronization is active. This explicit privacy action does not initialize ads. If the option is not shown or you need help with provider-held records, contact feridun.alacam@gmail.com. Device advertising/privacy choices can also be managed in system settings.

Do non-personalized ads collect no data?

No. Sync requests non-personalized ads. When ads are enabled, iOS may ask whether you allow tracking for optional ad measurement; declining keeps the core app available. Google does not receive the IDFA through Sync without permission. Its SDK can still process device/app identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics. Its publisher first-party ID default is retained. Sync does not pass account, routine, activity, backup or music data into ads. See In-app advertising and privacy choices for details.

06

Deleting your account

  1. If subscribed, manage or cancel renewal with your purchasing store first.
  2. In Sync, open Profile → Your account → Delete account.
  3. Sign in again if requested, then type DELETE and confirm the deletion.

If you used Sign in with Apple, Sync attempts to revoke that connection with Apple when a valid server credential is available. If the app shows that manual action is needed, open Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Sync and remove its connection, or follow Apple’s instructions (external website). Account deletion still completes if this additional Apple step is needed.

Successful deletion removes the Sync account and its related cloud application records. It does not automatically cancel a subscription or delete provider/store records. Device-local media copies can remain. See the privacy notice for the complete implementation boundaries.
